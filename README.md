## 🎯 About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was an eminent aerospace scientist, teacher, author, and the 11th President of India. Widely known as the **"Missile Man of India,"** he played a key role in India's missile and space programs. His vision, humility, and dedication to empowering young minds continue to inspire millions across the world.

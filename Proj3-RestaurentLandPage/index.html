Our Philosophy

Three Generations,
One Flame.

Fornace began in 1962 as a single wood-fired oven behind my grandmother's kitchen in Umbria. Every recipe we serve descends from her notebook — measured not in grams, but in how close the wood sits to the coals.

Today our chefs still source within sixty miles: heirloom tomatoes from Alder Hill Farm, flour milled weekly, and live oak cut and cured on-site for eighteen months before it ever touches the hearth.

Marco V.
Executive ChefThird generation, Fornace

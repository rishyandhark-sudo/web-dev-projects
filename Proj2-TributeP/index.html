Who They Were

A Life in Brief

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a humble family. Despite facing financial hardships during his childhood, he pursued his education with determination and earned a degree in Aerospace Engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology. He went on to become one of India's most celebrated aerospace scientists, playing a pivotal role in the country's missile and space development programs. His remarkable contributions earned him the title "Missile Man of India."

Beyond his scientific achievements, Dr. Kalam served as the 11th President of India (2002–2007) and became widely known as the "People's President" for his humility, wisdom, and close connection with students and young people. He dedicated his life to inspiring the next generation to dream big, embrace knowledge, and work for the nation's progress. His vision for a developed India, unwavering patriotism, and timeless words continue to motivate millions around the world even after his passing on 27 July 2015.

The Journey

A Life, Chronicled

  1. 1931

    Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu

    Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Raised in a humble family, he learned the values of hard work, discipline, and perseverance from an early age.

  2. 1960

    Started His Career as an Aerospace Engineer

    After graduating from the Madras Institute of Technology, Dr. Kalam joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO). Later, he became a key scientist at ISRO, contributing to India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III).

  3. 1980–1998

    Led India's Missile and Nuclear Programs

    Dr. Kalam directed the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, leading the development of Agni and Prithvi missiles. He also played a major role in the successful Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998, earning the title "Missile Man of India."

  4. 2002

    Became the 11th President of India

    Dr. Kalam was elected as the 11th President of India and served from 2002 to 2007. Loved for his humility, vision, and dedication to young minds, he became widely known as the "People's President."

  5. 2015

    A Legacy That Continues to Inspire

    On 27 July 2015, Dr. Kalam passed away while delivering a lecture at the Indian Institute of Management Shillong. He dedicated his final moments to teaching students, leaving behind a timeless legacy of knowledge, service, and inspiration for generations to come.

What They Left Behind

Contributions & Achievements

★

Missile Man of India

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam played a pivotal role in India's missile and defence technology programs. He led the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), resulting in the successful development of Agni and Prithvi missiles, strengthening India's defence capabilities.

🏆

Bharat Ratna & National Honors

Dr. Kalam received India's highest civilian award, the Bharat Ratna in 1997. He was also honored with the Padma Bhushan (1981) and Padma Vibhushan (1990) for his exceptional contributions to science, technology, and public service.

📚

Books That Inspire Millions

Dr. Kalam authored several best-selling books, including Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, India 2020, and My Journey. His writings continue to inspire students, educators, scientists, and leaders across the world.

🌐

An Everlasting Legacy

Remembered as the "People's President", Dr. Kalam dedicated his life to empowering young minds and promoting innovation. His vision for a developed India, humility, and commitment to education continue to motivate millions of people to dream big and serve the nation.

“Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.”

— Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, Wings of Fire

What is Fieldnotes?

Fieldnotes is a notebook for people who collect observations outside the office. Write text, attach photos and tag locations, then search everything later.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. You can create and edit notes offline. They sync automatically the next time your device connects.

Photos are saved on your device first, so a weak signal never blocks you from capturing them.

How much does it cost?

The Free plan includes up to 200 notes and 1 GB of photo storage. Pro costs $6 a month and removes both limits.

Students and teachers get Pro at half price. See pricing details.

Can I share a notebook with my team?

Open a notebook, choose Share, and invite people by email. You decide whether each person can view or edit.

How do I export my notes?

Go to Settings, then Export. You can download everything as Markdown with photos in a separate folder, or as a single PDF per notebook.

Is my data private?

Your notes are encrypted in transit and at rest, and only people you invite can open a shared notebook. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Export your notes first, because deletion is permanent after 14 days.

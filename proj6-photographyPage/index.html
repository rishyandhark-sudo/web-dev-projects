The Studio

One photographer, one darkroom, no digital intermediary between the shutter and the print.

Vesper Studio began as a single roll of Tri-X shot in an empty parking structure at 3am, printed by hand three days later in a borrowed darkroom. That first contact sheet — imperfect, grainy, honest — set the terms for everything since.

The work stays close to that origin: unposed, unhurried, shot on mechanical cameras that ask nothing of a battery. Every frame is exposed once, developed once, and printed with the mistakes left in, because the mistakes are usually the truest part of the image.

— M. Renard, Vesper Studio

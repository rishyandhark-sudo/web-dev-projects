About Us

A community built by students, for students.

Founded within Siddhartha Academy of Higher Education, the Innovate Club brings together engineers, designers, writers, and thinkers to work on projects that matter. We run workshops, hackathons, guest talks, and social meetups all year round — no experience required, just curiosity.

Every member gets hands-on mentorship, a supportive peer network, and the chance to lead initiatives that shape campus life.

Emergency Care

Our emergency department operates 24/7 with a dedicated trauma team, rapid triage protocols and direct access to imaging and surgical theaters for life-threatening conditions.

Trauma Care24/7 ResponseAmbulance Service

Cardiology

From routine ECGs to angioplasty and cardiac rehabilitation, our cardiology team manages heart health across every life stage with a focus on prevention.

ECG & EchoAngioplastyCardiac Rehab

Stroke CareEpilepsyNeuro-imaging

Our orthopedic surgeons treat fractures, joint conditions and sports injuries, with a strong physiotherapy program to support full recovery.

Joint ReplacementSports InjuryPhysiotherapy

VaccinationsGrowth ChecksNewborn Care

Skin ScreeningLaser TreatmentAcne & Eczema
